## Open and use the candidate

Open `/projects` and choose **Development Kit**, or open `/projects/development-kit` directly. Existing route authentication is retained. The new workspace does not authorize confidential customer files or a hosted tenant service.

1. Copy a publicly shareable AOI from the active saved Create project. Saved geocontext requires its own unchecked opt-in; it is not refreshed. Alternatively, deliberately open a labelled synthetic example. Missing saved data never loads a synthetic replacement automatically.
2. Apply the brief. Evidence, mapped objects, claims, unknowns and constraints remain distinct. The study-area diagram is not a cadastral map. Saved Find results can be copied explicitly from the same active owner/project and market; no new source request runs.
3. Generate the two conceptual programmes, then edit the selected building. Historical versions remain immutable. Restoring an earlier design creates a new current revision; changing A does not rewrite B. Critical unknowns block generation unless the user explicitly accepts a conceptual draft retaining those gaps.
4. Prepare an area schedule for the current design. Enter monthly financial assumptions, or explicitly use the synthetic arithmetic example. An empty value stays unknown; a synthetic amount does not become market evidence.
5. Save on this device, then prepare the kit from the exact saved snapshot. Brief edits, changed sources or geometry invalidate dependent results. Unsupported calculation methods are not presented as validated numeric conclusions.

## What is preserved

The versioned project includes the brief, AOI, separate spatial objects, evidence and rights, claims, constraints, design history, area and finance references, and procedure metadata. Local JSON export/import and reopen preserve these values; they are not cloud backup or cross-device synchronization. The local store is limited to 768 KiB per project, 2 MiB overall and 12 projects. Retain an external JSON copy before clearing browser data or changing Preview hostname.

Existing owner-scoped readers and strict parsers supply the transfer. Local identity namespaces are not server-side tenant isolation. Conflicting writes preserve the last saved version and require reconciliation; they do not silently merge two edits.

## Model and calculation boundaries

- Geometry is an editable conceptual model, with an approximate local metric frame, not BIM or an approved planning proposal. Retained buildings and exclusion zones use supplied geometry. Unknown existing heights are not invented.
- Area definitions remain separate: footprint, enclosed gross area, terrace and manual allocation inputs are not cadastral registered or permitted floor area.
- `monthly-available-cash-v1` dates row 0 at the valuation instant and subsequent rows one month apart. Effective annual discount rates are converted to monthly factors. Restricted escrow enters available project cash when released. Interest is based on opening principal; current-row draws begin accruing the next row. Project and equity flows are separate. The synthetic three-month example is an arithmetic demonstration, not a forecast of the selected geometry's return.
- Regional routes contain 20 Dubai and 10 Moscow procedure records with primary references and gaps. Attaching them does not establish the actual plot authority, applicant mandate, title, zoning or permission. Researcher-authored metadata is included; restricted raw forms and records are not bundled.
- Full-kit export checks source rights. If those rights are unresolved, a separate metadata-only draft is available: it is labelled, omits original content and cannot be imported as a project. Neither export means professional review or submission.
