## Matrix contract

The five tables below form **one product-keyed 25-field matrix**. Every product key appears once in every table. The matrix deliberately separates hosted services from open-source components and separates customer-input ownership from generated-output and post-termination rights.

Cell prefixes are controlled vocabulary:

- `PC` — published-confirmed in a current first-party source;
- `VC` — first-party vendor claim that has not been independently validated;
- `CQR` — contract, order form, quote or written supplier confirmation required;
- `UNK` — unknown or not verified in this research wave;
- `N/A` — not applicable to this product or component.

No `PC` or `VC` cell proves UAE planning compliance, commercial suitability or acceptance by a pilot customer. `UNK` is a deliberate fail-closed value, not an implied negative.

## Shortlist and decision use

The first identical UAE-oriented bake-off remains intentionally limited to three to five routes:

1. `GEOAI-REF` as the bounded deterministic baseline;
2. `TF-SITE` as the direct commercial benchmark, subject to desktop workflow and written rights;
3. `ADSK-SITE`, with `ADSK-BLDG` tested only where schematic-building detail/native Revit handoff is material;
4. one composable route: `HYPAR-ELEM` or `RHINO-COMP`;
5. `ESRI-URBAN` only when a named customer already has a usable Urban/ArcGIS model; `ESRI-CITY` may be added for a procedural-AOI brief.

All other rows remain components, later-layer partners or watchlist options. No row is vendor-approved. No supplier may advance based on graphics alone, an open-source label, a public API page or a sales claim. Promotion requires written commercial rights, applicable security/region evidence, local data/rule evidence, full marginal cost and better performance than `GEOAI-REF` on the same customer decision.
